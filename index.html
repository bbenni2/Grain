Features

Built for photographers
who actually shoot.

  1. 01

    AI auto-culling

    Every photo is scored for sharpness, exposure, and composition. The best frames rise to the top — automatically.

    Local AI
  2. 02

    Smart organisation

    Date-based folders, consistent naming, and XMP sidecars with labels and keywords — ready for Lightroom or Capture One.

    Lightroom-ready
  3. 03

    Privacy first

    Zero cloud. Zero accounts. Zero telemetry. The AI model runs on your Mac — your photos never leave it.

    100% Private
  4. 04

    Every RAW format

    RW2, CR2/3, NEF, ARW — plus JPEG and PNG. Grain reads your camera's files directly. No conversion, no plugins.

    RAW + JPEG
  5. 05

    Presets & grading

    Default, cinematic, bright & airy, muted earth — colour grading via LUT, plus vignette and film grain. Or roll your own.

    LUT grading
  6. 06

    Watcher mode

    Grain watches for SD cards and mounts, then runs the whole pipeline hands-free. Plug in, walk away.

    Automatic
